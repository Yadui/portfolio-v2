# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, and technical clients evaluating Abhinav Yadav's cloud, AI, and full-stack engineering work. They are scanning quickly on desktop and mobile and need to understand capability, credibility, and how to make contact.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Abhinav's engineering practice through selected work, experience, skills, writing, and a direct contact path. Success means a visitor can understand the role and strongest proof within the first viewport, then move naturally to work samples or contact without waiting through ornamental UI.

## Brand Personality

Bright, technical, and considered. The experience should feel confident and crafted, with editorial spacing, real evidence, and motion that supports hierarchy rather than delaying access to content.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing-page patterns, long blocking loaders, centered dark mesh heroes, decorative maps that compete with the message, fake dashboards, excessive cards, and motion that delays the first useful view.

## Design Principles

- Show the work quickly. The first viewport should be useful without an intro sequence.
- Make the portfolio itself proof of care through typography, composition, and responsive polish.
- Balance technical credibility with a distinct visual point of view.
- Keep interactions purposeful, reversible, and fast to understand.
- Preserve direct paths to work, résumé, writing, and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, avoid blocking content behind animation, preserve meaningful heading structure and alt text, and keep the hero usable at narrow mobile widths.
